Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026. UMBRA Mundo is a personal, non-commercial project that creates short educational videos in Spanish about world news and curious facts. This policy explains how the UMBRA Mundo app uses data when connected to TikTok.

1. Data we access: when the channel owner connects the app with TikTok Login, the app receives only the basic profile information and the permissions the owner explicitly approves (for example, permission to upload or publish videos to the owner's own account).

2. How we use it: only to upload and publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. We do not access, collect, or analyze data about other TikTok users.

3. Storage and sharing: we do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties. Access tokens are kept only as long as needed to publish and can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings under Security and permissions.

4. Videos: the videos are created by an automated tool and contain no personal data of third parties.

5. Children: the service is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

6. Your rights: the owner can disconnect the app and request deletion of any stored data at any time.

7. Contact: via the TikTok profile of UMBRA (UMBRA Mundo).
